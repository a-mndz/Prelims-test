# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Programming competition participants taking a timed, single-attempt exam on desktop, and administrators reviewing anti-cheat signals and results during a live round.

## Product Purpose

Run a fair, reliable preliminary round without losing participant state. Participants must understand exam status, remaining time, saved-answer state, and submission consequences at a glance. Administrators must review evidence without confusing client-side deterrents for security guarantees.

## Brand Personality

Calm, rigorous, focused. Voice is direct and operational, especially during errors or irreversible actions.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic purple-gradient startup UI, overdecorated dashboard templates, excessive cards, glass effects, ornamental metrics, and interchangeable corporate styling.

## Design Principles

- Keep question content and remaining time visually dominant.
- Make save, connection, and submission state explicit.
- Prefer familiar controls and clear labels over novel interactions.
- Use color only for action, selection, warning, and status.
- Treat anti-cheat UI as transparent detection, never intimidation or false security.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic controls, live status announcements, sufficient contrast, reduced motion, reflow at narrow desktop widths, and state cues that do not rely on color alone.
